# 2. Planning and tracking

What gets done in which order, and what was decided along the way.

1. **One ordered backlog.** Work is picked up from the top; the order is the owner's.
2. **A check-in with the owner every three to five finished items:** a quick confirmation
   or a reordering.
3. **Three records, each with one job:** the backlog (what is still to do), a status
   record (what is done; a finished item moves there), and a decision log (the questions
   asked and the answers given).
4. **No dates or names on rules and backlog items;** the history is in version control.
   Dates of measurements stay ("measured on 2026-10-12").
5. **Backlog items link to the rules they apply instead of repeating them.**
6. **One subject per change, in small steps.** A change that needs another one merged
   first says so in its title ("merge after ...").
7. **Planning notes that contain personal information stay in a private place,** apart
   from anything published.
